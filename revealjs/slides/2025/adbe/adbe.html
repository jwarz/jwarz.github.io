TEXT AND IMAGE TO VIDEO

Video workflows, reinvented.

Pick two still images and use them as keyframes to generate a video. Or put your idea into motion with a text prompt. Generate b-roll, create visual effects, and refine shots by choosing camera angles, motion, and style.

AUDIO AND VIDEO TRANSLATION

Go global. Sound local.

Make anyone sound like a native speaker by maintaining the same voice, tone, and cadence across multiple languages.

Explore new ways to create (2/2)

 

STYLE AND STRUCTURE REFERENCE

Create with more precision.

Get the right look in less time, even without the perfect prompt. Mimic layouts, poses, and styles. Adjust and refine, or try a new reference image to explore more creative options.

SCENE TO IMAGE

Put your images into perspective.

Design dimensional brand graphics, packaging, illustrations, and more in an intuitive 3D workspace. Create accurate spatial relationships, change angles, and adjust lighting fast — no redrawing needed.

CUSTOM MODELS

Train on your own brand assets with Custom Models.

Drive on-brand content creation across your organization with custom models integrated into your Adobe solutions, unleashing your teams’ creativity while consistently maintaining your unique look and feel.

Campaign refreshes

Accelerate campaign refreshes by streamlining repetitive tasks.

Quickly produce mass quantities of asset variants and keep marketing up to date — without overburdening your creative team.

  • Easily resize assets for any channel or platform.
  • Quickly update seasonal campaigns with Firefly-generated imagery.
  • Scale on-brand content quickly with Custom Model APIs.

Content localization

Localize assets and videos to increase marketing reach.

Go global with fast, easy-to-use localization that scales.

  • Save time and effort using AI-generated location backgrounds instead of creating local variants from scratch.
  • Streamline content assembly workflows by automating time-consuming, repetitive tasks.
  • Reduce production costs with Translate and Lip Sync APIs to convert videos into multiple languages.

Content personalization

Personalize assets and videos to connect with customers.

Create unlimited asset variations that tailor your content to diverse audience segments—breaking past resource limits so you can captivate like never before.

  • Generate Firefly assets variations without overwhelming your creative teams.
  • Incorporate segment- specific messages with relevant images into Photoshop templates to personalize ads at scale.
  • Generate and test more assets to enhance campaign performance.

AI-driven experiences

Create unique user experiences to wow customers.

Encourage brand interaction through personalized content, powered by AI.

  • Increase engagement by building individual, customized experiences.
